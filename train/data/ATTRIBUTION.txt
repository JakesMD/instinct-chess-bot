Derived data notice
====================

The .npy files in this archive (train_x.npy, train_y.npy, val_x.npy,
val_y.npy) are a tokenized re-encoding of the ChessBench dataset released by
Google DeepMind:

  Source:  https://github.com/google-deepmind/searchless_chess
  Paper:   Ruoss et al., "Grandmaster-Level Chess Without Search"
           (DeepMind, 2024, arXiv:2402.04494)
  License: data released under CC-BY (see the searchless_chess repo for the
           authoritative license terms)

Changes made: raw (FEN, move, win-probability) records were converted from
the original .bag format into fixed-length tokenized uint16/float32 arrays
via instinct_chess_bot/convert_bag_to_npy.py in this repo. No content was
added or removed beyond this re-encoding.

This archive is a redistribution of a derivative of that dataset under the
same CC-BY terms; attribution to Google DeepMind / the ChessBench authors
must be preserved by downstream users.
